Bargainu: three UI/UX prototypes

Bargainu (バーゲイヌ, bargain + 犬) collects discounts from Japanese online stores in one place. These are three takes on the finished v1 app. Each has the same five screens and the same fake data, and only the design recipe differs. Pick the one we build on.

What to try in each

  • Home: find the Top Discounts section and open a deal.
  • Browse: combine filters (discount %, brand, category, online vs in-store, prefecture and city), then clear them.
  • Deal detail: save a deal. You will be asked to sign in.
  • Login: use the demo sign-in. There is no real account and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Favorites: check the saved deal is there, then remove it to see the empty state.
  • Repeat on your phone.
All deals, brands and prices are invented. Photos are from Unsplash. Store names are labels only. How each one was built is recorded in docs/design/recipes/ in the repo.
